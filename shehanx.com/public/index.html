About me

I turn real problems into working products.

I'm a self-driven developer from Galle, Sri Lanka. My work started with a practical need — my own shop needed a better point-of-sale system — and grew into Swift, a software company with three products.

I enjoy the whole journey: understanding how people actually work, designing simple screens, writing the code, wiring up the cloud and AI, and supporting users after launch. I care about speed, clean design and getting the small details right — like a receipt that prints perfectly on a 55 mm thermal printer.

Alongside Swift, I build automation tools, AI agents and custom business systems for clients.
